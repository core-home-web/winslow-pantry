Good to know

Where can I buy Winslow Pantry?

Winslow Pantry is on its way to stores. Send us a note and we will let you know where to find it near you.

Is the silicone utensil rest BPA free?

Yes. It is BPA free, heat resistant up to 425°F (218°C) and dishwasher safe.

How do I care for wood and bamboo?

Hand wash, dry right away and skip the dishwasher. A little food safe oil every few weeks keeps the wood smooth and looking its best.

Can I read this site in another language?

Yes. Use the language menu at the top of any page to switch between English, Spanish and French.
