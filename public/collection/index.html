5 piece set

Bamboo Utensil Set

Five bamboo tools for stirring, flipping and serving.

Smooth, lightweight bamboo that feels good in the hand. The set covers nearly everything you cook in a week, from Sunday pancakes to a big pot of chili.

Features

  • Natural bamboo
  • Gentle on cookware
  • Five everyday shapes

What's included

  • Slotted spoon
  • Spoon
  • Spork
  • Turner
  • Slotted turner

Care

Hand wash in warm, soapy water and dry right away. Rub with a little food safe oil when the wood looks thirsty.

With juice well

Acacia Cutting Board

All natural acacia wood with a groove that catches every drop.

Slice a ripe tomato or carve a roast chicken without juice running onto the counter. The groove around the edge keeps things tidy, and the warm acacia grain makes the board handsome enough to serve on.

Features

  • All natural acacia wood
  • Juice well
  • Hanging hole
  • Engraved Winslow Pantry mark

What's included

  • One acacia cutting board with juice well

Care

Hand wash only. Do not soak it or put it in the dishwasher. Oil now and then to keep the wood looking rich.

Holds up to 4 utensils

Silicone Utensil Rest

A tidy place to park your spoons while you cook.

Rest up to four utensils at once and keep sauce off the stovetop. Flexible silicone sits steady on the counter and rinses clean in seconds.

Features

  • BPA free
  • Prevents messes
  • Heat resistant up to 425°F (218°C)
  • Dishwasher safe

What's included

  • One silicone utensil rest with four slots

Care

Dishwasher safe. You can also wash it by hand in warm, soapy water.

3 piece set

Silicone Funnels

Three sizes for jars, bottles and spice tins.

Fill a mason jar with homemade jam, pour olive oil into a bottle or top up the salt cellar without a spill. The three sizes nest together, so they take up almost no room in the drawer.

Features

  • Three sizes
  • Nest for storage
  • Flexible silicone

What's included

  • Small funnel
  • Medium funnel
  • Large funnel

Care

Wash in warm, soapy water and let them dry before nesting.

10 piece set

Magnetic Bag Clips

Keep snacks fresh and clips handy on the fridge.

Seal chips, coffee, flour and frozen peas, then stick the clips to the fridge so they are always close by. Ten clips in five easygoing colors: oat, wheat, lake blue, navy and stone.

Features

  • BPA free
  • Durable design
  • Magnetic back
  • Multi-use

What's included

  • 10 magnetic clips in 5 colors

Care

Wipe clean with a damp cloth.
